TinyKit is a small, fast collection of Android system tweaks and tools powered by
Shizuku. No root, no ads, no analytics, and nothing ever runs in the background: every
change is written straight into Android's own settings or app-ops and then it is done.

Pure black UI, organised into a bottom bar (Shizuku, Privacy, Apps, System, Info) with a
search over every tool.

Quick Settings tiles: Caffeine, Developer options, USB debugging, Hide developer options,
Sensors off, Grayscale, Extra dim, Ad-block DNS, Mic and camera off, Screen timeout,
Clear caches, Free RAM, Close the app on screen, Screenshot, Screen record.

Privacy: set any app's permissions to Real, Fake (shown as granted but the app gets
nothing) or Off; turn off every sensor; stop Huawei and Google analytics and HiCloud;
view saved Wi-Fi passwords and share them as QR codes.

Apps: hide apps from the launcher, pick default apps, find unused apps, see storage by
app and recent sensor access, and play apps' audio together or mute an app.

System: force dark on apps that have no dark mode, edit the quick panel, run a command,
recover deleted files (with thumbnails) from every recycle bin, and back up or restore
settings.

Info: battery, network (with public IP and a shortcut to your router's settings), charging,
temperatures, device IDs, battery usage, wake-ups, vibration and app logs — all as readable
lists.

Requires Shizuku running. Free and open source (MIT).
